Current Transaction Procedures — Review Before Buyer Access

Seller-provided procedures for the five current offers. Seller identity and direct source contact information remain withheld. Buyers should select only a structure their company, bank, terminal and logistics providers can perform.

EN590 10PPM — Vopak-to-Vopak — Rotterdam / Houston

View Vopak-to-Vopak procedure
  1. Buyer submits CIS and issues ICPO for seller verification and approval, identifying the buyer's Vopak facility/name.
  2. Seller issues Commercial Invoice (CI); buyer signs and returns it within 24 hours.
  3. Seller authorizes the buyer's Vopak Terminal Manager to communicate directly with the seller's Vopak Terminal Manager to verify product existence.
  4. After seller-terminal confirmation, buyer's Vopak Terminal Manager submits its Vopak Terminal Agreement/reference and Vopak barcode.
  5. Buyer completes the applicable Vopak terminal protocols.
  6. Seller releases Full POP to the buyer's Vopak Terminal Manager and injection begins.
  7. After completed injection, buyer pays by MT103/TT and seller transfers title.
  8. Only direct Vopak lease holders are accepted; JV/group, proxy and sublease structures are not accepted under this offer.

EN590 10PPM — Tank-to-Tank FOB

View tank-to-tank procedure
  1. Buyer submits ICPO with company registration and TSA. If the buyer has its own tank farm, the tank-farm name is submitted for seller review.
  2. Seller issues Commercial Invoice (CI) for the available quantity; buyer signs and returns it.
  3. Seller issues Dip Test Authorization (DTA), endorsed by the relevant parties including the buyer's tank farm. The DTA includes the tank number and hub number.
  4. Seller issues the stated PPOP package, including recent SGS, TSR/tank-storage documents, port-authority authenticity documentation, origin/authority documents and export license.
  5. Buyer orders SGS Q&Q in seller's tank at buyer expense.
  6. After satisfactory Q&Q, buyer submits the required TSR/ATV/SGS documentation and seller issues the final CI/title documentation.
  7. Buyer pays by MT103/TT and product injection into the buyer's secured tank proceeds under the stated procedure.
  8. This offer excludes Vopak and certain other named operators and also states that a tank-extension structure may be used.

EN590 10PPM / Jet A-1 / D6 — Supplier Tank to Buyer Tank

View supplier-tank-to-buyer-tank procedure
  1. Buyer issues ICPO incorporating the seller procedure together with TSA, KYC/CIS, representative identification and banking details.
  2. After buyer-company, bank and TSA review, seller issues Commercial Invoice (CI).
  3. Buyer signs and returns the CI and provides MT799/MT199 Proof of Funds by SWIFT as stated in the offer.
  4. Seller provides the stated POP package, including DTA, injection report, TSR with GPS coordinates, tank-farm barcode information, commitment to supply, registration/export documentation, ATSC, injection schedule, ATV and fresh SGS Q&Q.
  5. Buyer verifies the product/POP documents and orders SGS to conduct the dip test in the seller's tank at buyer expense.
  6. Following a successful dip test, buyer pays by MT103 within 24–48 hours and product injection into the buyer's designated tank proceeds.
  7. Seller transfers title of ownership into the buyer's name and issues the title-ownership certificate.

Sulfur & Urea — CIF

View CIF procedure
  1. Buyer issues LOI; seller responds with SCO.
  2. Buyer issues ICPO including the banking and corporate information required by the seller procedure.
  3. Seller issues draft SPA; the parties review and execute the transaction documentation, including the stated transaction-protection agreement.
  4. Seller issues the stated PPOP package, including origin, registration, availability, product passport/specifications, commitment to supply and proforma invoice.
  5. After PPOP verification, buyer provides the financial instrument or guarantee step required by the seller procedure within the stated banking period.
  6. After confirmation of the instrument/deposit, seller issues the stated 2% performance bond and Full POP package and proceeds with shipment.
  7. At the discharge port, buyer coordinates SGS/CIQ inspection.
  8. Following favorable destination inspection, buyer settles by MT103/TT under the SPA and the annual contract program continues after successful first shipment.

Important: The seller procedure also contains a stated $500,000 logistics-security deposit alternative if the required financial instrument is not issued within the specified period. Buyers should review the full redacted offer before proceeding.
