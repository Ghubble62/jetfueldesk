Transaction Procedures — Review Before Buyer Access

Seller-provided transaction structures, shortened for readability without removing the principal buyer requirements. Seller identity and direct source contact information are intentionally withheld. Choose only a procedure your company can perform.

CIE098 — FOB / CIF Procedure Options

Applies to the products and FOB ports represented in the current CIE098 offer. The seller document provides multiple transaction structures; the applicable structure must be identified in the buyer's requirement/ICPO.

01 — FOB Dip Test / Tank Injection
  1. Buyer issues ICPO incorporating the seller procedure, banking details and company profile.
  2. After ICPO verification, seller issues Commercial Invoice (CI); buyer countersigns.
  3. Seller releases the stated POP package.
  4. Buyer reviews/inspects SGS and provides its Tank Storage Receipt (TSR).
  5. NCNDA/IMFPA is executed for the transaction participants.
  6. Buyer conducts the authorized dip test; upon successful result, product is injected into buyer storage.
  7. Buyer pays by MT103 in exchange for title.
02 — FOB Tank Takeover
  1. Buyer issues ICPO using the seller working procedure.
  2. Seller issues CI for product in tank; buyer signs and returns it.
  3. Seller provides the stated product/tank documentation for verification.
  4. NCNDA/IMFPA is executed by the transaction participants.
  5. Buyer performs the required verification/dip test and, after satisfactory Q&Q, proceeds under the tank-takeover/payment sequence stated in the seller procedure.
03 — FOB Tank Terminal Clearance / Tank Takeover
  1. ICPO submission and buyer-company documentation.
  2. Seller issues Commercial Invoice.
  3. Seller provides Partial Proof of Product (PPOP).
  4. Buyer completes the required Tank Terminal Clearance Permit (TTCP) step.
  5. Dip Test Authorization is issued and physical verification proceeds.
  6. Inspection report and vessel nomination are completed as applicable.
  7. Tank Takeover Agreement and required payment step are completed.
  8. Title is transferred in accordance with the procedure.
  9. NCNDA/IMFPA is issued at the stage specified in the seller procedure.
04 — FOB Vessel-to-Tank (VTT)
  1. Buyer issues ICPO/Purchase Order and Tank Storage Agreement (TSA).
  2. Seller issues CI; buyer signs and returns it.
  3. Seller releases the stated POP for the loaded/arriving vessel.
  4. Buyer provides the receiving/logistics documentation required by the procedure.
  5. Vessel berths at buyer's nominated port/terminal.
  6. After successful Q&Q/dip test, buyer pays by wire transfer/SWIFT MT103.
  7. Seller releases original POP/title documents and trans-loading proceeds.
  8. Intermediary commissions are paid and the parties proceed with the contract program.
05 — FOB Tank-to-Tank (TTT)
  1. Buyer provides the required corporate and tank/storage documentation.
  2. Seller issues CI and the applicable Tank-to-Tank Injection Agreement (TTTIA).
  3. Buyer/logistics company executes the TTTIA; terminal/pipeline authentication and service-billing steps follow as required.
  4. Seller releases the stated product documentation.
  5. Buyer completes terminal/product verification and injection.
  6. Following satisfactory inspection and successful injection, buyer pays under the stated transaction terms.
06 — FOB Tank-to-Tank / SGS Before Payment
  1. Buyer issues ICPO with TSA and the buyer documentation required by the procedure.
  2. Seller issues CI for product in tank; buyer signs and returns it.
  3. Seller provides SGS copy and NDA for buyer/tank-farm verification.
  4. Buyer/tank farm verifies SGS and product in seller tank.
  5. Buyer conducts SGS dip test at buyer expense.
  6. Seller issues authorization to transfer product from seller tank to buyer tank.
  7. Buyer pays by MT103 after completed injection and receipt of the required transfer/product documentation.
07 — FOB Rotterdam Tank-to-Tank
  1. Buyer issues ICPO with full banking details, company profile and CIS.
  2. Seller issues CI; buyer signs/seals and returns it with acceptance within the stated period.
  3. Buyer provides a verifiable TSA from a suitable tank farm.
  4. Buyer and seller complete the stated tank-extension/storage-day arrangement with the tank farm.
  5. Seller releases the stated PPOP documentation.
  6. Seller begins injection into buyer storage.
  7. After injection, seller issues Dip Test Authorization; buyer conducts SGS Q&Q/dip test.
  8. After successful inspection, buyer pays the product value by MT103.
  9. Seller transfers title after receipt of payment; commissions and the contract program follow.
08 — FOB Tank-to-Vessel (TTV)
  1. Buyer issues ICPO and the required vessel/logistics documentation.
  2. Seller issues CI and Tank-to-Vessel Injection Agreement (TTVIA).
  3. Buyer/logistics parties execute the TTVIA and complete terminal authentication/service requirements.
  4. Seller releases the stated product documentation.
  5. Buyer completes terminal clearance, product verification and injection into the nominated vessel.
  6. Following satisfactory inspection and successful injection, buyer pays under the stated transaction terms.
09 — FOB Title Takeover (TTO)
  1. Buyer issues ICPO with CIS/KYC and the financial evidence required by the seller procedure.
  2. Seller issues draft SPA and CI; buyer reviews/signs as required.
  3. Seller issues the stated POP documentation in buyer-company name.
  4. Buyer supplies shipping-agent information for operational coordination.
  5. Buyer makes the guarantee-deposit/payment step required by this specific TTO procedure.
  6. Seller and buyer execute title-transfer documentation and title is issued in buyer name.
  7. Vessel/shipping documents are released through the stated channel.
  8. At destination, CIQ/inspection is completed and buyer completes the remaining payment obligation.
  9. Intermediary commissions are paid under the transaction structure.
10 — CIF Procedure
  1. Buyer issues ICPO with CIS/KYC and the financial-capability evidence required by the CIF procedure.
  2. After buyer verification, seller issues CI and SPA.
  3. Buyer signs/seals and returns CI/SPA.
  4. Seller releases the stated Partial POP.
  5. Buyer establishes the required DLC/SBLC or applicable bank/payment guarantee within the stated banking period.
  6. After activation/verification of the instrument, seller releases Full POP and proceeds with shipment.
  7. At the designated discharge port/anchorage, inspection is completed and payment proceeds according to the CIF contract.
  8. Subsequent monthly shipments use the revolving/renewable instrument and documentation sequence stated in the contract.
