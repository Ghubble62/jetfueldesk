Jet Fuel Desk

Privacy Policy

Jet Fuel Desk collects information that buyers, sellers and authorized representatives voluntarily submit through our website so we can review potential petroleum transactions, communicate with submitters and evaluate possible counterparty introductions.

Information we collect

Submitted information may include company and representative details, contact information, product requirements or offers, quantities, delivery locations, transaction terms, storage or terminal information, and documents you choose to upload.

How information is used

We use submitted information to review eligibility and commercial fit, request clarification or supporting documentation, compare potential counterparties, and facilitate an introduction when appropriate. We may also retain records reasonably necessary for business, compliance, dispute-resolution or legal purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell submitted personal or corporate information as a data product. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to evaluate or facilitate a potential transaction, with service providers that support website or form operations, when you authorize disclosure, or when required by law. We seek to limit transaction-related disclosure until there is a legitimate business reason to share it.

Security and sensitive information

We use the website and hosting services available to us to receive and manage submissions, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit passwords, online-banking credentials, private keys, or other account-access credentials through the forms. Where direct bank, storage, terminal, inspection or asset verification is needed, the parties may be asked to arrange verification directly through the relevant institution or source.

Third-party services

The site may rely on third-party hosting, form-processing, email or other service providers. Their processing may be governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Your responsibility

Only submit information and documents that you are authorized to provide. Each party remains responsible for its own due diligence before entering a transaction.

Contact

Questions about a submission or this policy may be sent to info@jetfueldesk.com.
